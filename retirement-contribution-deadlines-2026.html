Retirement Contribution Deadlines for Tax Year 2026

Most tax-year decisions can still be corrected on the return. Retirement contributions are the exception, and the exception is not the same for every account. One deadline passes with the calendar year; the other waits until April.

Two deadlines, not one

Workplace deferrals — the percentage of each paycheck routed into a 401(k) or 403(b) — are tied to payroll. They stop when the payroll year stops. Individual accounts, meaning a traditional or Roth IRA, and a health savings account if you have an eligible plan, follow the tax return: money for tax year 2026 can still go in until the filing deadline in April 2027. So the useful question is not whether you maxed out, but which account and which deadline it follows.

What closes on December 31, 2026

Workplace deferrals must come out of pay during the tax year. A contribution elected in January 2027 belongs to tax year 2027, even if the intent was to catch up. The action is therefore a payroll change: adjust the deferral percentage in time for at least one remaining pay date, then confirm it on the final stub of the year.

  • Change the percentage, not the account — payroll reads the election
  • Confirm on the last stub of the year, not the first one after the change
  • Two employers in one year mean two plans; neither can fix the other

What can wait until the filing deadline

IRA contributions for tax year 2026 can be made until the federal filing deadline in April 2027, and the deposit is designated for 2026 when you make it. HSA contributions follow the same pattern if you were eligible during the year. Both need the money deposited by that date; an intention does not count, and the provider has to code it to the right year.

What belongs in the packet

What a preparer needs is proof of what was contributed for the year, and the timing of any payroll election.

  • The final pay stub of 2026, showing year-to-date deferrals
  • The year-end plan statement, if it arrives before you file
  • IRA and HSA confirmations with the year they were coded to

Form 5498 is issued after the filing season, so waiting for it is not a plan. The packet should be complete without it.

Starting with tax year 2027

The Saver's Credit is replaced by the Saver's Match for tax years beginning after 2026: a match-style benefit for eligible lower- and moderate-income savers, claimed on the return. It changes the shape of the incentive, not the deadlines above. For anyone near the income range, what is contributed for 2027 is what gets tested, so next year's payroll election is the one to review.
