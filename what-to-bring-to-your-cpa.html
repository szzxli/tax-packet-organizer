What to Bring to Your CPA: A One-Page Intake Sheet

A preparer's first question is not about your income. It is about how much of the packet is already in order. One page beats a folder, because a folder makes them ask the same six questions they ask every client.

The page, in five sections

Keep it to one side of a page. Anything longer stops being read and becomes another document to sort.

  • Who: filing status, dependents, states you lived in and the dates
  • Last year: a copy of last year's return, or the form numbers and payers if you do not have the copy
  • This year's income: one line per source, with the form number and the payer, and a mark for what is still missing
  • What changed: one line per change, and the document each change produces
  • Questions: the three things you actually want answered, written before the meeting

What belongs in the packet, not on the sheet

The sheet is an index. The forms, statements and receipts go behind it, in the same order as the lines on the sheet. If the preparer has to hunt for the support behind line four, the sheet has failed.

Send the index first, the documents second

Email the one-page sheet before the meeting and bring the documents with you, or upload them to whatever secure portal the firm uses. Do not email a full return, a Social Security number or account numbers. A preparer who sees the index in advance arrives with the right questions instead of a blank form.

Mark what is missing before you hand it over

A line you know is incomplete is worth more than a line you guessed at. Mark it, name what is missing, and say when you expect it. Preparers price work by how much has to be chased; a packet that declares its own gaps is faster and cheaper to review.

Why a structure list instead of a shoebox

Nothing here decides whether a document is correct, and nothing here computes anything. The index only answers one question: is the paper for each line present, filled in, counted and supported. That is the question a preparer asks first, and it is the one you can answer before the meeting.
