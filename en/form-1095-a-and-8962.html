Form 1095-A and Form 8962: How They Pair Up

These two forms do different jobs and only make sense together. One reports what your Marketplace plan cost and what was paid toward it during the year. The other reconciles that with what you were entitled to. If you had Marketplace coverage, both belong in the packet.

What Form 1095-A actually reports

The 1095-A is the Health Insurance Marketplace Statement. It comes from the Marketplace, not from the insurer, and it is issued for the coverage year. It lists who was covered, the months of coverage, the monthly premium, the benchmark plan premium for your area, and any advance payment of the premium tax credit that went straight to the insurer.

  • Recipient and covered individuals, with the months each was covered
  • Monthly enrollment premiums
  • Monthly second lowest cost silver plan (SLCSP) premium
  • Monthly advance payment of the premium tax credit

What Form 8962 does with it

Form 8962 is the reconciliation. It compares the advance credit that was paid on your behalf with the credit your actual household income and family size support. If too much was paid, the difference is repaid; if too little, the difference is claimed. The arithmetic needs the monthly figures from the 1095-A, which is why the pair is filed together.

The three things to check before you hand both over

Most problems with this pair are clerical, and they are cheap to catch early.

  • The year on the 1095-A matches the return you are filing
  • Every month of coverage is listed, including months with a zero premium
  • The people listed match the people on the return, including a child who turned 26 mid-year or a spouse added after a life event

If the 1095-A is missing

It is not mailed to everyone who had Marketplace coverage. Sign in to your Marketplace account, open the tax forms section, and download the 1095-A for the coverage year. If you switched plans or moved mid-year, you may have more than one. If your coverage came through an employer, a 1095-B or 1095-C is the document you have instead, and it plays a different role.

If the numbers do not line up

Do not adjust a figure to make it fit. A mismatch between the advance credit and the 8962 is a question for a preparer, not a rounding problem. Flag it, keep the 1095-A, and let the person reviewing the packet decide. Structure first, numbers second.
